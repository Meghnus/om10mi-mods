Your pack

This is the Picture Mode menu order: the menu, the SCP picker, the Live Control strip and dial stepping. Drag the grip, use the arrows, or focus a row and press Alt+Up / Alt+Down. Edit a menu name in place; the counter is the capacity of that slot. i-Enhance stays first. Hide is not available in this release.

    Export

    The sha1 pins the exact release image on your PC; the builder refuses any other image (never use an image of another camera model).

    Build on your PC

    1. Save the pack as next to looktool/om10mi_look.py.
    2. Put your own release image and the LUT files used here into folders on your PC.

    The builder validates the pack, checks the image sha1, writes slots, names and order, audits the result and runs the acceptance battery; a failing step builds nothing. It never writes to a camera or a card.
