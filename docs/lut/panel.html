Look list manager → E-M10 Mk I look pack om10mi-lut panel, model cm-v2-web1

Everything runs in this page. Your LUTs and photos are read from disk into memory and never uploaded (CSP: no external connections). The page writes a pack file (look list order, names, and our own fitted numbers); the image itself is built later on your own PC by looktool/om10mi_look.py --pack from your own copy of the firmware. Scores are model predictions, not camera results. Single-LUT page: index.html.

Disclaimer. Unofficial, for the Olympus OM-D E-M10 Mark I (model 0x1130, firmware 1.4) only. This page never talks to a camera and does not contain, produce or distribute any firmware. Turning a pack into a firmware image is done on your own PC with your own copy of the official firmware (om10mi-mods); flashing a modified image is at your own risk, can make the camera unusable and voids support. The sha1 pins the exact release image on your PC; the builder refuses any other image (never use an image of another camera model). The sha1 of the version 94 image is preselected; the builder prints it when it finishes (om10mi.py build).








2. Look list (Picture Mode order)


Order of the Picture Mode menu, the SCP picker, the Live Control strip and dial stepping. Drag the handle, use the arrows, or focus a row and press Alt+Up / Alt+Down. Greyed rows are stock items (movable, not editable). The eye (hide) is disabled: hiding has not been proven on a camera, so the builder refuses it. Names are written into the menu; the counter shows the capacity of the slot. i-Enhance is always first and cannot move.
