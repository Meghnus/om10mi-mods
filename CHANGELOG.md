# Changes

## 56 (current release)

The builder produces this version; the result must have sha1 `0d4e1649cb930d39fc49a331d92840a7cbcbd83e`.

New in 56 compared with 51:
- Four new looks: Portra 400, Kodachrome 64, Ektar 100, Gold 200.
- Per-bank Contrast, Sharpness, Saturation, Gradation and Sector/Hue/Sat/Light rows in RAW Data Edit; Highlight,
  Shadow, Contrast and Gradation now act on looks.
- Colour-named Sector, row titles and captions, new icons.
- Contrast and Sharpness in the SCP menu work on all looks.
- Acros is black and white again in RAW Data Edit Custom.

Carried over from earlier versions:
- 51: looks selectable in RAW Data Edit Custom1/2.
- 48: per-look colour editor on the Custom page.
- 46: per-sector colour editor (Hue/Saturation/Lightness).
- 40: video recording limit 59:50.
- 37: 14 film looks with their own colour matrix and tone curve.

After installing: User Hue > Reset, once.
